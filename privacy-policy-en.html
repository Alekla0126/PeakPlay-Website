Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Introduction

At PeakPlay NFL ("we", "our" or "the app"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information we may collect through our mobile application. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Contact information when you reach out to us
  • Comments and feedback you voluntarily share
  • App preferences and settings

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device information (model, operating system, version)
  • App usage data (features used, time spent)
  • Performance and crash data used to improve the app
  • IP address and general location information (country/region)

2.3 Third-party data

  • Sports data from the ESPN API (public NFL information)
  • Advertising information from Google AdMob

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the functionality of the app
  • Display up-to-date and relevant sports content
  • Improve and optimize app performance
  • Personalize your user experience
  • Show relevant advertising through Google AdMob
  • Analyze usage in order to improve our services
  • Communicate with you when necessary
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

4. Sharing Information

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties, except in the following cases:

  • Service providers: Google AdMob for advertising, ESPN for sports data
  • Legal compliance: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets
  • With your consent: When you give us specific permission

5. Advertising and Google AdMob

Our app uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect information about your device and app usage in order to show personalized advertising.

  • You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings
  • Google AdMob is subject to its own privacy policy
  • Advertising helps keep the app free for everyone

For more information about Google's privacy practices, visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Access to personal information limited to authorized personnel only
  • Regular monitoring of our security systems
  • Regular security updates

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request information about the data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured format
  • Objection: Object to the processing of your data for certain purposes
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at: privacy@peakplay.app

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

  • Usage data: 2 years from last activity
  • Contact information: Until you request its deletion
  • Crash data: 90 days for technical analysis
  • Server logs: 30 days for security and performance

9. Underage Users

Our app is designed for general audiences and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately.

10. International Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure that such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws and that appropriate safeguards are in place.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by publishing the new policy in our app and on our website. Your continued use of the app after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the new policy.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us:

  • Email: privacy@peakplay.app
  • Support: support@peakplay.app

By using PeakPlay NFL, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.
